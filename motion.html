PIR Technology

The Passive Infrared (PIR) sensor detects motion by measuring the infrared light radiating from objects in its field of view.

  • ✅ Detection Angle: 120 Degrees
  • ✅ Range: Up to 7 Meters
  • ✅ High sensitivity to human body movement
  • ✅ Adjustable delay and sensitivity
